# 2. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the operational methodology, leadership responsibilities, coordination workflows, escalation management procedures, and communication standards for SOC Lead incident coordination activities.

The SOC Lead is responsible for centralized coordination of security incidents across:

- L1 analysts
- L2 analysts
- L3 analysts
- Incident Response Team (IRT)
- IT Operations
- Network teams
- Cloud teams
- MSSP operations
- Executive stakeholders

SOC Lead coordination ensures incidents are:

- Properly prioritized
- Escalated appropriately
- Investigated efficiently
- Contained rapidly
- Communicated clearly
- Documented accurately
- Resolved within SLA expectations

Poor incident coordination may result in:

- Delayed containment
- Missed escalations
- Duplicate investigative effort
- Operational confusion
- SLA violations
- Communication failures
- Regulatory reporting delays
- Business disruption

This SOP ensures:

- Structured incident management
- Centralized operational oversight
- Effective inter-team coordination
- Timely escalation handling
- Consistent communication workflows
- Audit-ready coordination records

# 4. SOC Lead Coordination Philosophy (IMPORTANT)

The SOC Lead acts as the operational command and coordination point during incidents.

The SOC Lead must maintain:

- Situational awareness
- Incident prioritization
- Escalation visibility
- SLA awareness
- Communication discipline
- Operational continuity

The SOC Lead is responsible for ensuring:

- The right teams are engaged
- The right severity is assigned
- The right actions occur at the right time
- Operational confusion is minimized
- Executive awareness is maintained

SOC Leads must avoid:

| Poor Practice | Operational Risk |
|---|---|
| Delayed escalation | Increased attacker dwell time |
| Weak communication | Operational confusion |
| Poor prioritization | SLA breaches |
| Lack of ownership clarity | Investigation gaps |
| Missing stakeholder updates | Executive escalation failures |
