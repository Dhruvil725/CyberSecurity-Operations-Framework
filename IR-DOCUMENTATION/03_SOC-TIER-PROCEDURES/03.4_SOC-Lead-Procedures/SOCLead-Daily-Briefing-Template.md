# 2. Purpose

This document defines the operational methodology, reporting standards, communication structure, and template requirements for SOC Lead daily operational briefings.

The daily SOC briefing is a structured operational communication mechanism designed to provide leadership, analysts, and stakeholders with visibility into:

- Current threat landscape
- SOC operational status
- Incident activity
- Escalations
- SLA performance
- Detection trends
- Operational risks
- Resource constraints
- MSSP client impacts

The SOC Lead is responsible for ensuring the briefing provides:

- Accurate situational awareness
- Operational transparency
- Executive visibility
- Prioritized action tracking
- Risk communication
- Shift continuity support

Improper daily briefings may result in:

- Operational blind spots
- Missed escalation awareness
- Weak executive visibility
- SLA tracking failures
- Resource coordination issues
- Inconsistent incident prioritization

This procedure ensures:

- Standardized operational briefings
- Consistent communication structure
- Clear escalation visibility
- Audit-ready reporting records
- Improved operational coordination
- Effective leadership awareness

# 4. Daily Briefing Philosophy (IMPORTANT)

The daily briefing is not simply a status update.

The objective is to provide:

- Operational awareness
- Threat visibility
- Escalation awareness
- Resource visibility
- SLA transparency
- Decision-making support

The SOC Lead must ensure the briefing is:

- Concise
- Accurate
- Action-oriented
- Operationally relevant
- Risk-focused

The briefing must prioritize:

1. Critical incidents
2. Operational risks
3. SLA concerns
4. Emerging threats
5. Resource constraints
6. Action ownership

SOC Leads must avoid:

| Poor Practice | Operational Risk |
|---|---|
| Excessive technical detail | Audience confusion |
| Missing escalation updates | Operational gaps |
| No prioritization | Misaligned focus |
| Weak action tracking | Accountability gaps |
| Missing SLA visibility | Compliance issues |
