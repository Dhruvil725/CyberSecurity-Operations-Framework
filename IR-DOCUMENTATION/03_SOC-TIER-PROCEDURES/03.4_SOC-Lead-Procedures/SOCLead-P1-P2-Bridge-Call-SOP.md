# 2. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the operational methodology, communication standards, coordination workflows, participant responsibilities, and escalation procedures for SOC Lead-managed P1 and P2 incident bridge calls.

Bridge calls are centralized communication and coordination sessions used during major cybersecurity incidents to ensure:

- Real-time operational coordination
- Rapid decision-making
- Stakeholder alignment
- Escalation management
- Containment coordination
- Executive visibility
- SLA compliance
- Accurate incident tracking

Bridge calls are critical during:

- Active ransomware incidents
- Data exfiltration events
- Domain compromise
- Cloud infrastructure compromise
- Critical business outages
- MSSP multi-client incidents
- Active attacker presence
- Regulatory-impacting incidents

Improper bridge call management may result in:

- Operational confusion
- Delayed containment
- Conflicting remediation actions
- Duplicate investigative effort
- Escalation failures
- Poor executive communication
- SLA breaches
- Incomplete documentation

This SOP ensures:

- Structured bridge call management
- Clear communication standards
- Defined participant responsibilities
- Consistent escalation workflows
- Audit-ready coordination records
- Effective incident leadership

# 4. Bridge Call Management Philosophy (IMPORTANT)

The bridge call is the central command-and-control communication mechanism during major incidents.

The SOC Lead is responsible for:

- Maintaining operational order
- Driving incident progression
- Coordinating technical teams
- Ensuring communication discipline
- Managing stakeholder expectations
- Tracking action ownership
- Maintaining timeline accuracy

Bridge calls must prioritize:

- Clarity
- Accuracy
- Timeliness
- Accountability
- Operational coordination

SOC Leads must avoid:

| Poor Practice | Operational Risk |
|---|---|
| Unstructured communication | Operational confusion |
| No action ownership | Missed tasks |
| Excessive technical detail for executives | Leadership confusion |
| Missing status cadence | Visibility gaps |
| No timeline tracking | Incident reconstruction issues |
