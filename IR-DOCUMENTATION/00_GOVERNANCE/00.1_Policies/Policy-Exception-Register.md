## 2. Purpose

This register records approved exceptions to the Incident Response Policy and related IR governance documents.

Exceptions may be required due to:
- Legacy technology limitations
- Client contractual constraints (MSSP)
- Operational constraints
- Temporary risk acceptance during projects/migrations

All exceptions must be:
- Risk assessed
- Time bound
- Approved by authorized stakeholders
- Reviewed periodically

---

## 3. Scope

This register applies to:
- Enterprise internal environments
- MSSP managed client environments
- All IR policy and supporting SOPs/playbooks

---

## 4. Exception Request & Approval Rules

### 4.1 Minimum required fields for an exception
- Policy requirement being exempted
- Business justification
- Risk rating and impact
- Compensating controls
- Start date and end/expiry date
- Approval signatures
- Review schedule

### 4.2 Approval authority (example)
| Exception Risk | Minimum Approval Required |
|---------------|----------------------------|
| Low | SOC Lead + Asset Owner |
| Medium | SOC Manager + Risk Owner |
| High | CISO + Compliance |
| Critical | CISO + CEO/Board Representative (as applicable) |

## 6. Review and Closure Process

### 6.1 Review
Exceptions must be reviewed:
- Quarterly (minimum)
- Upon major incident
- During audits or regulatory inspections

### 6.2 Closure
An exception can be closed when:
- Original requirement is met
- System is upgraded/decommissioned
- Compensating controls are replaced with permanent controls
- Expiry date is reached and exception is not renewed

Closure must include:
- Closure date
- Evidence of remediation
- Approver confirmation

---

## 7. Audit Notes

For audits (ISO 27001 / NIST / RBI), the following should be provided:
- List of active exceptions
- Risk acceptance approvals
- Compensating controls evidence
- Review records
- Closure evidence
