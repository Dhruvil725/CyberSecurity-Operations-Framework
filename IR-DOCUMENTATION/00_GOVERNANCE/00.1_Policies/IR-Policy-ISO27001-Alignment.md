## 2. Purpose

This document defines how the organization's Incident Response Program aligns with:

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- ISO/IEC 27002:2022 (Controls Guidance)

This alignment ensures compliance with Information Security Management System (ISMS) requirements related to incident management.

## 5. Incident Lifecycle under ISO 27001

The organization follows structured phases:

1. Detection
2. Recording
3. Assessment
4. Response
5. Recovery
6. Documentation
7. Improvement

Each phase is documented within:

- Playbooks
- SOC Procedures
- Evidence & Reporting folders
