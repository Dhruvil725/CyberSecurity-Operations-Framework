## 2. Role Purpose

Management provides governance, approvals, and business coordination during security incidents to ensure:

- Business impact is assessed and controlled
- Incident response actions align with risk appetite
- Stakeholders are informed appropriately
- Regulatory and contractual obligations are met
- Resources are available for response and recovery
- Post-incident corrective actions are funded and executed

---

## 3. Reporting Structure

Management roles may include:
- SOC Manager / Head of SOC
- Security Manager / Head of Security Operations
- IT Operations Manager / Infrastructure Manager
- Application Owners / Business Service Owners
- Compliance Manager (as applicable)

## 4. Key Responsibilities

### 4.1 Governance & Oversight
- Ensure IR Policy is implemented and maintained
- Ensure SOC operations have defined roles, procedures, and SLAs
- Approve incident response processes and playbooks
- Support audits and compliance reporting (ISO/NIST/RBI)

### 4.2 Major Incident Decision-Making
For P1/P2 incidents, management must:
- Participate in incident bridge call (or delegate decision maker)
- Approve high-impact actions where required, such as:
  - isolating critical servers
  - disabling business-critical accounts
  - shutting down services
  - restricting network connectivity
  - invoking DR/BCP processes
- Ensure risk-based decision making is documented

### 4.3 Resource Mobilization
- Assign required IT/network/cloud resources for containment/recovery
- Authorize overtime/on-call activation as needed
- Approve engagement of third-party vendors / IR retainers (if required)

### 4.4 Regulatory / Legal / Client Coordination
- Ensure Compliance and Legal are engaged early when required
- Ensure contractual client notification requirements are met (MSSP model)
- Support regulator-required reporting (RBI/CERT-In) through authorized channels

### 4.5 Communication & Stakeholder Management
- Ensure executive summaries are produced for leadership
- Ensure communications are consistent and approved
- Prevent unauthorized communications that create legal or reputational risk

### 4.6 Post-Incident Accountability
- Ensure Post Incident Review (PIR) is conducted
- Ensure corrective actions are tracked and closed
- Ensure budgets and owners are assigned to remediation tasks
- Ensure improvements to monitoring and controls are implemented

Reference:

---

## 5. Decision Authority

Management MAY:
- Approve containment and recovery actions that impact business services
- Invoke BCP/DR plans
- Approve engagement of external specialists/vendors
- Approve regulatory reporting initiation (via Compliance/CISO)
- Approve risk acceptance or exceptions (documented)

Management MAY NOT:
- Conduct hands-on forensic actions unless authorized and trained
- Issue public statements (handled by approved communications/legal team)
- Override regulatory reporting obligations

---

## 6. Required Skills / Competencies

- Risk assessment and decision making under pressure
- Understanding of business-critical systems and dependencies
- Knowledge of incident severity and response lifecycle
- Stakeholder management and communication
- Awareness of regulatory and contractual obligations (RBI/ISO/NIST)

---

## 7. Key Deliverables

- Incident decisions and approvals documented in tickets/bridge notes
- Business impact assessment confirmation
- Resource allocation confirmation
- Post-incident corrective action approvals
- Management review reporting (monthly/quarterly)

---

## 8. KPIs / Governance Metrics

- Response decision time for P1/P2 approvals
- SLA compliance oversight
- Remediation action completion rate post-PIR
- Audit findings related to incident management
- Reduction in repeat incidents due to improvements

---

## 9. Review & Update

This role definition shall be reviewed:

- Upon organizational restructure
- After major incidents / PIR outcomes
